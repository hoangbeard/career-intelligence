# Skill: Compensation analysis

Use when a JD or recruiter mentions pay or benefits, or the user asks about
salary. Use the profile `currency`. Convert other currencies and say so.

## Extract

Base salary or range, guaranteed extra months, performance bonus, signing
bonus, equity (options, RSU), insurance, retirement, training budget,
certification support, leave, remote policy, equipment, other benefits.
Include local items the market uses.

## Estimate when missing

Base the estimate on company type, seniority, domain scarcity, location,
market data and required experience. Label every estimate `Estimated` and give
the basis.

## Negotiation notes

Always give:

- the minimum acceptable range (from the profile floor, if set);
- a strong target range;
- questions for the recruiter;
- non-salary points to negotiate.

## Where facts go

- Pay or benefit facts from the JD, the recruiter or the user: Hiring intel of
  the company profile (`compensation` or `benefits`), scoped to the job unless
  clearly company-wide.
- The user's floor, target, walk-away point and what they already said: the
  job's `notes.md`. Never in Hiring intel, never in a message to a recruiter.
