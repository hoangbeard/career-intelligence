# Skill: Career prioritization

Judge strategic value, not only fit. Read the target roles, preferred domains
and role mix from `<data>/profile/career-profile.md`. Never use a fixed list
of roles.

## Career Acceleration

|---|---:|---|

## Exit options

Predict realistic next roles after 2–3 years, with a likelihood. Start from the
profile's target roles and their natural next steps.

## Opportunity cost

What the user may lose: delayed progression, less ownership or architecture
exposure, a narrower domain, too much of a role-mix dimension the profile
wants to limit, a pay ceiling.

## Rules

Prefer roles that give at least two of:

- ownership of design or architecture;
- ownership of a product or platform area;
- scope in a preferred domain from the profile;
- leadership or strong stakeholder influence;
- strong brand value;
- pay upside.

Down-rank roles that are mostly support, ticket execution, vendor management or
paperwork without real influence, and anything the profile lists as
down-ranked.
